Instructions

Five minutes from plugging in to seeing your playing as notes and tab.

What you need

  • A guitar, plugged into an audio interface with a cable, or played into a microphone
  • Chrome on a computer (Safari often blocks audio inputs)
  • The index.html file from the project folder, opened on your computer, not the preview on claude.ai

Play live

  1. Open the studio. Open index.html in Chrome.
  2. Start listening. Click Start listening and choose Allow when Chrome asks for the microphone.
  3. Pick your input. Choose your interface from Input. Leave Channel on Both inputs unless you know your guitar is on Input 1 or 2.
  4. Check your level. Strum and watch the meter. It shows your level in dB. If it says too quiet, turn up your interface's gain or slide Sensitivity left.
  5. Tune up. Play one open string. The big letter shows the note; the dot on the tuner sits in the teal middle when you're in tune.
  6. Play single notes. Each note appears as tab on the easiest string and fret, and as a colored dot in the trail. Chords come in a later version.
  7. Read the circle. After six or more notes, the circle of fifths lights up your likely key, and the chips below show the chords that fit it.

Other ways in

Try a demo
Plays a short built-in melody so you can see everything work without a guitar.
Play a recording
Drop a WAV from Audacity on the box at the bottom, or click Choose an audio file. It plays out loud and transcribes as if you were playing live.

Settings

Tuning
Standard, Drop D, half step down, Open G or DADGAD. The tab's string names follow it.
Capo
The fret your capo is on. Tab numbers count from the capo.
Sensitivity
Left picks up quieter playing; right ignores more background noise.
Copy tab / Clear
Copy the tab as text to paste anywhere, or start fresh.
The title
Click it to move the sand to its next stage. While you play, it follows your notes.

If something's wrong

Nothing happens when I click Start listening
Click the icon at the left of Chrome's address bar and allow the microphone. On a Mac, also open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and switch on Google Chrome, then restart Chrome.
The meter moves but no notes appear
Play one note at a time and let it ring. Use a clean sound: distortion, reverb and delay confuse note detection.
Chrome still won't use my interface
In Terminal, go to the project folder and run python3 -m http.server 8000, then open http://localhost:8000 in Chrome.
It feels slow
Close other heavy tabs. A smaller window also helps on very large screens.
